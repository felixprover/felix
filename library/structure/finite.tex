\section{Finite structures}

\import{set/finite.tex}

A structure is finite precisely when its carrier is finite.  This reading is
kept separate from ordinary set-finiteness, so complete phrases such as
``finite set'' and ``finite group'' select their intended domains explicitly.

\begin{definition}\label{finite_structure}
    Let $X$ be a onesorted structure.
    $X$ is finite iff $\carrier[X]$ is a finite set.
\end{definition}
