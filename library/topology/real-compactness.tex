\section{Compactness on the real line}

\import{topology/order-compactness.tex}
\import{topology/real-order-topology.tex}

The generic compact-interval theorem applies literally to the established
real topology, since its order and metric presentations are equal.

\begin{theorem}\label{real_closed_interval_is_compact}
    Let $X$ be a topological space.
    Suppose $X=\RealTopology$.
    Let $a,b\in\reals$.
    Then $\realClosedInterval{a}{b}$ is compact in $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    $\RealField$ is a conditionally complete linearly ordered set by
        \obv{surreal_real_conditionally_complete_linear_order}.
    $a,b\in\carrier[\RealField]$ by
        \obv{surreal_real_field_carrier}.
    Hence $\orderClosedInterval{\RealField}{a}{b}$ is compact in
        $\orderTopology{\RealField}$ by
        \obv{order_closed_interval_is_compact}.
    $\orderClosedInterval{\RealField}{a}{b}
        =\realClosedInterval{a}{b}$ by
        \obv{real_order_closed_interval_eq}.
    $\orderTopology{\RealField}=\RealTopology$ by
        \obv{real_order_topology_eq_metric_topology}.
    Hence $\orderTopology{\RealField}=X$.
\end{proof}
