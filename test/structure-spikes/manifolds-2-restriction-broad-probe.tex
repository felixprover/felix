This diagnostic keeps an explicit input structure but enlarges the import
closure. Its chart-membership statement must form before the proof is checked.

\begin{theorem}\label{imported_restricted_charts_at}
    Let $G$ be a coordinate pseudogroup and $X$ be a topological space.
    Let $S$ be a relative coordinate structure on $X$ under $G$.
    Let $U\in\opens[X]$.
    Then $c\in\ChartsAt{\RestrictedCoordinates{G}{X}{S}{U}}{p}$ iff
        $c\in\atlas[S]$ and $\dom{c}\subseteq U$ and $p\in\dom{c}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    $\RestrictedCoordinates{G}{X}{S}{U}$ is a coordinate datum by
        \auto{restricted_coordinates,relative_coordinate_structure,is_open}.
    Follows by \auto{charts_at_iff,restricted_coordinates_chart_iff}.
\end{proof}
