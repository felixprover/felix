\begin{definition}\label{formation_probe_admissible}
    Let $n$ be a natural number and $Y$ be a topological space.
    $Y$ is formation-probe-admissible of dimension $n$ iff $Y=Y$.
\end{definition}

    Suppose for every $j\in I$ we have $X(j)$ is formation-probe-admissible of dimension $n$.
