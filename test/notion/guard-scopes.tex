% Readable design example; executable counterparts live under test/phase9.

% The checked antecedent licenses the consequent while it is compiled.
If $X$ is a topological space, then $X$ is compact or it is wrong that
    $X$ is compact.

% The implication as a whole exports no topological-space assertion.
$X$ is compact.

% A positive conjunction may be projected after it becomes a local fact.
Assume $Y$ is a topological space and $Y$ is inhabited.
$Y$ is compact or it is wrong that $Y$ is compact.
